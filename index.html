Features

Everything you need to read a profile.

Four simple tools that turn a long list of submissions into something you can actually learn from.

01

Rating graph

See every rated contest as one smooth line. Hover any point to read the contest name and the rating you reached.

02

Tag analysis

Find your strongest topics. A bar chart ranks the ten tags you have solved the most, from dp to graphs.

03

Solved problems

Duplicate accepted submissions are removed. Search by name, filter by tag or rating range, and sort by rating or newest.

04

Saved handles and bookmarks

Keep the handles you follow and the problems you want to solve later. Everything stays after you refresh the page.
